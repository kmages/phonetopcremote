Control Your PC – Delete your data

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Control Your PC (from Doromind) has no accounts and does not collect your screen, keystrokes or personal details. The only data tied to you is an anonymous app identifier and your subscription status, held by our subscription service, RevenueCat. This page explains how to have that removed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email kmages+support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Control Your PC data".
  2. Include the Google Play order number or Apple receipt/order number for your subscription (from your purchase receipt email), so we can find your record. We do not need your name or any password.
  3. We will confirm by reply and ask RevenueCat to delete the record within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • The anonymous app user identifier and subscription status stored with RevenueCat for your purchase.

What is not deleted by us

  • Payment and billing records kept by Apple or Google under their own policies and tax law. Contact them to manage those.
  • Data on your own phone and computer (your computer's address and, if you chose "Keep me connected", saved login). Press Close in the app, or delete the app, to remove it from your phone; run the uninstall script to remove the program from your computer.

Deleting your record does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

See also the Privacy Policy.
